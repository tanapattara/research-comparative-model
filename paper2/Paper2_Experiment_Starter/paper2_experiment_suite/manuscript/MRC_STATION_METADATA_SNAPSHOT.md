## Datum interpretation boundary

The bulletin labels these values as zero gauges above mean sea level. They are **station-specific gauge datums**. The five numbers must not be interpreted as observations expressed relative to one verified common vertical datum, must not be subtracted to infer channel slope or water-surface gradient, and must not be used to infer travel time. This snapshot records the official dynamic-page metadata only; it does not harmonize national levelling networks or establish cross-station vertical comparability.
