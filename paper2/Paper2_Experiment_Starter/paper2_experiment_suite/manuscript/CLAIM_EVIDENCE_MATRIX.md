# Claim–evidence matrix

## Classification rule

- **Confirmatory** — directly supported by the frozen E0–E5 evidence within its
  declared protocol and limitations. This label does not imply prospective
  external confirmation.
- **Exploratory** — useful descriptive or sensitivity evidence that must not be
  promoted to a primary confirmatory claim.
- **Unsupported** — not established by the frozen evidence; manuscript wording
  must state `UNSUPPORTED` or omit the claim.

All paths are relative to this `manuscript/` directory.
